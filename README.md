# NOWN 🌐

### See what's happening now.

NOWN is a modern, real-time social discovery platform designed to help people discover **events, activities, meetups, deals, communities, and spontaneous experiences happening around them.**

Instead of searching across multiple platforms to figure out what's happening nearby, NOWN brings local activity into one place through a **live discovery feed and interactive map**.

> **Discover your city. Find your people. Join the moment.**

---

## 🚧 Project Status

**Currently in development.**

The project is currently in the **product planning and UI/UX development stage**.

The initial focus is building the core discovery experience, followed by real-time social features, recommendations, organizer tools, and business features.

---

## ✨ Core Concept

NOWN answers one simple question:

> **"What's happening around me right now?"**

Users will be able to discover things happening nearby based on:

* 📍 Location
* 🎯 Interests
* 🔥 Trending activities
* 👥 People they follow
* ⏱️ Real-time activity
* 📅 Upcoming events
* 🗺️ Distance

## 🚀 Planned Features

### 🗺️ Real-Time Discovery

Explore activities and events happening around you through an interactive map.

* Live activity markers
* Nearby events
* Distance-based discovery
* Search this area
* Map filters
* Live activity indicators

---

### 🔥 Live Activity Feed

Discover what's happening right now.

Examples:

* 🎵 Concerts
* 🏀 Pickup sports
* 🎮 Gaming tournaments
* 🍔 Food events
* ☕ Meetups
* 🎨 Workshops
* 🎓 Campus activities
* 💻 Tech meetups

---

### 👥 Social Discovery

See what people around you are doing.

Planned features include:

* Follow users
* Friends attending events
* Social proof
* User profiles
* Activity history
* Event-specific communities

---

### 💬 Real-Time Event Chat

Each event can have a temporary live chat.

Users can:

* Send messages
* React to messages
* Share images
* Ask questions
* Interact with other attendees

The goal is to make events feel alive before and during the experience.

---

### 🙋 Join Activities

Users can quickly join an event using:

**I'm In**

After joining:

**✓ You're Going**

Users can then see the activity inside their upcoming events.

---

### 📍 Check-In

Users can check into activities when they arrive.

Possible check-in mechanisms:

* QR codes
* Geofencing
* Organizer confirmation

Check-ins can also contribute to the NOWN gamification system.
